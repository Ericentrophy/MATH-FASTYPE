How to play
  1. Choose your grade (Grade 1–6, High School, or College) and a challenge below.
  2. Pick how long to play (30 seconds up to 3 minutes), then press Play Blitz.
  3. Type each answer on your keyboard, or tap the number pad on a phone or tablet. It checks itself, so you don’t need to press Enter or Go.
  4. Correct answers earn coins that move you up the ranks. Practice has no timer, lets you choose 12, 30, 50 or unlimited questions, and a coach gives hints and explains mistakes.
  5. Hints (Practice only): tap 💡 up to 3 times. Hint 1 is a nudge, hint 2 is a closer clue, hint 3 shows the full steps. On a computer press H. Blitz has no hints, so practise first.
  6. After every round you see which answers were right or wrong, why, and your pace (speed vs target).
  7. Campus challenges: the Daily Challenge gives everyone the same questions today. A challenge code lets friends or a whole class play identical questions, then compare scores.
  8. Math toolbox: calculator, fraction helper, percent helper, unit converter, equation solver, formula sheet, study timer and a printable worksheet maker for teachers and tutors.
  9. Fixed number and question timer: for tables challenges you can keep one number fixed on the left (like 4 × ?). Open ⚙ Options to set the time per question: 3s, 5s, 10s, your own number of seconds, or No timer. When time runs out the question counts as missed and the answer is shown.
  10. Speedrun restart: press R or Ctrl+R on a computer, or tap the ↻ Restart button on a phone to instantly restart the same challenge. Works during a run and on the results screen.

1 Math grade / level

Pick Grade 1–6 for elementary, or High School / College for advanced pace. Tap one and the page jumps to step 2. Use Custom for full control.

2 Choose a challenge

Tap a challenge and the page jumps to the next step. Challenges depend on your grade.

3 Pick your tables

A “times table” is the list for one number. The 5 table is 5 × 1, 5 × 2, 5 × 3 and so on. Choose a level, or pick your own numbers. Then press Play.

Fixed number (optional)

Pick one number to keep on the left of every question, like 4 × ?, while the number on the right changes. In Division the fixed number is what you divide by. Choose Off for a mix of tables. The Daily Challenge and challenge codes ignore this so everyone gets the same questions.
