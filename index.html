How to play
  1. Choose a challenge below.
  2. At the bottom, pick how long to play (30 seconds up to 3 minutes), then press Play Blitz.
  3. Type each answer. It checks itself, so you don’t need to press Enter.
  4. Correct answers earn coins that move you up the ranks. Practice has no timer.

Pick your tables

A “times table” is the list for one number. The 5 table is 5 × 1, 5 × 2, 5 × 3 and so on. Tap a level to start, or choose your own numbers.
